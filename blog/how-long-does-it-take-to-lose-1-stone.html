How Long Does It Take to Lose 1 Stone?

Aug 11, 2026 5 min read

Losing one stone (14 pounds) typically takes 7 to 14 weeks, or approximately 2 to 3.5 months, when you aim for a healthy and sustainable weight loss rate of 1-2 pounds per week. This timeframe allows for gradual, consistent progress that is more likely to be maintained long-term, rather than through rapid, unsustainable methods.

What is a Healthy Rate of Weight Loss?

Experts generally recommend a weight loss rate of 1 to 2 pounds (approximately 0.5 to 1 kg) per week. This seemingly modest pace is crucial for several reasons:

  • Sustainability: Slower weight loss is often more sustainable because it allows for lifestyle changes that can be maintained over time, rather than drastic, temporary diets.
  • Muscle Preservation: When you lose weight too quickly, you risk losing valuable muscle mass along with fat. Maintaining muscle is important for metabolism and overall strength.
  • Fewer Side Effects: Rapid weight loss can lead to issues like fatigue, nutrient deficiencies, gallstones, and a rebound effect where lost weight is quickly regained.
  • Better Adherence: Gradual changes are easier to stick with, reducing the likelihood of burnout or giving up.

Since one stone equals 14 pounds, losing 1-2 pounds per week means it would theoretically take 7 to 14 weeks to achieve this goal. However, individual results can vary based on numerous factors.

What Factors Influence How Quickly You Lose Weight?

The journey to losing a stone isn't linear, and several personal factors can influence your individual timeline:

  • Starting Weight: Individuals with a higher starting weight often see quicker initial weight loss, as their bodies have more to lose and require a larger caloric deficit to maintain their current weight.
  • Metabolism: Your basal metabolic rate (BMR) – the number of calories your body burns at rest – varies greatly from person to person due to age, gender, genetics, and body composition.
  • Dietary Habits: The quality and quantity of your food intake are paramount. A consistent caloric deficit, achieved through nutrient-dense foods, is key.
  • Physical Activity Level: Regular exercise burns calories and builds muscle, both of which support weight loss. The type, intensity, and frequency of your workouts play a significant role.
  • Sleep Quality: Poor sleep can disrupt hunger hormones (ghrelin and leptin) and increase cravings for high-calorie foods, making weight loss more challenging.
  • Stress Levels: Chronic stress can lead to elevated cortisol levels, which can promote fat storage, particularly around the midsection.
  • Medical Conditions & Medications: Certain health conditions (like hypothyroidism) or medications can affect metabolism and make weight loss more difficult. Always discuss these with a healthcare provider.
  • Consistency and Adherence: The most significant factor is your ability to consistently stick to your plan over time.

Strategies for Sustainable Stone Loss

Achieving your goal of losing one stone requires a holistic approach focused on sustainable lifestyle changes rather than quick fixes. Here's how to set yourself up for success:

1. Create a Moderate Caloric Deficit

Achieving a healthy weight loss rate of 1-2 pounds per week fundamentally depends on creating a consistent calorie deficit, where you consume fewer calories than your body burns. A deficit of 500-750 calories per day is generally recommended to achieve the 1-2 pounds per week loss. Focus on nutrient-dense foods to ensure you get essential vitamins and minerals while reducing calories.

  • Prioritize Protein: Focusing on dietary protein for weight loss can significantly support muscle preservation and increase satiety, making it easier to adhere to a calorie-controlled eating plan over the 7-14 week period needed to lose one stone.
  • Increase Fiber Intake: Foods rich in fiber (fruits, vegetables, whole grains) promote fullness, aid digestion, and help regulate blood sugar levels.
  • Limit Ultra-Processed Foods: These are often high in unhealthy fats, added sugars, and sodium, contributing to excess calorie intake and poor nutrient density.

2. Stay Active Consistently

Combine both cardiovascular exercise and strength training for optimal results.

  • Cardio: Activities like brisk walking, jogging, cycling, or swimming burn calories and improve cardiovascular health. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity cardio per week.
  • Strength Training: Incorporating strength training workouts is vital to preserve muscle mass during weight loss, helping to maintain a healthy metabolism and ensuring that a greater proportion of the weight lost is fat. Aim for 2-3 sessions per week.

Even increasing non-exercise activity thermogenesis (NEAT) – like taking the stairs or walking more throughout the day – can significantly contribute to your daily calorie burn.

3. Prioritize Sleep and Manage Stress

Don't underestimate the impact of these two factors:

  • Quality Sleep: Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night. It helps regulate appetite hormones and improves decision-making, making it easier to stick to your healthy eating plan.
  • Stress Management: Practice stress-reducing techniques such as meditation, yoga, deep breathing, or spending time in nature.

4. Hydrate Adequately

Drinking plenty of water can help you feel full, boost your metabolism, and reduce cravings for sugary drinks. Sometimes, thirst can be mistaken for hunger.

5. Track Your Progress and Be Patient

Weight loss is rarely a straight line. You'll have good days and challenging days. Tracking your food intake, activity levels, and weight can provide valuable insights and keep you accountable. Remember that plateaus are normal, and consistency is more important than perfection.

The Takeaway: Consistency Over Speed

Losing one stone is a significant and achievable goal. While the exact timeline will vary, focusing on a healthy rate of 1-2 pounds per week means you could reach your target in approximately 2 to 3.5 months. Prioritize sustainable habits, listen to your body, and remember that patience and consistency are your greatest allies.
