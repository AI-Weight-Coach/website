Adult DRI for Vitamin D
Daily Recommended Intake (Vitamin D)600-800 IU (15-20 mcg) per day for adults (up to 70 years old)
Food Sources (Vitamin D)Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, tuna), cod liver oil, fortified milk/cereals/orange juice, egg yolks. Primary source is sunlight exposure.
Deficiency Symptoms (Vitamin D)Fatigue, muscle weakness/pain, bone pain, increased risk of fractures, impaired immune function, mood disturbances (e.g., depression).
Evidence for Weight Loss (Vitamin D)Observational studies show a correlation between lower Vitamin D levels and higher BMI/obesity. However, most interventional studies do not show significant weight loss from Vitamin D supplementation alone in individuals who are not deficient, though it may support overall metabolic health.
Supplementation Needed (Vitamin D)Often needed for individuals with limited sun exposure, darker skin, malabsorption conditions, or those who consume few dietary sources. A blood test can determine deficiency.

Does Essential Vitamins for Mood and Energy Help with Weight Loss?

Vitamins crucial for mood and energy, like Vitamin D, play a foundational role in overall health, which indirectly supports a healthy weight. While Vitamin D deficiency is often observed in individuals with obesity and can impact metabolic health, current evidence does not strongly support Vitamin D supplementation as a direct weight-loss strategy for those who are not deficient. Focusing on a balanced diet rich in essential nutrients and addressing any underlying deficiencies is key for both mood, energy, and weight management.
