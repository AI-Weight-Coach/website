Is the Best Diet Adjustments for a Weight Loss Plateau Diet Right for You?

Weight loss plateaus are a normal physiological response as your body adapts to lower calorie intake and reduced body mass. The 'diet adjustments' approach focuses on refining existing healthy habits, identifying overlooked variables (like calorie creep or insufficient activity), and recalibrating your energy balance. It's not a new diet but a strategic troubleshooting guide, which is highly effective when implemented with patience and precision.
