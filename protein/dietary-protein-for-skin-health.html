Is Adequate Dietary Protein Intake for Skin Health also beneficial for Weight Loss?

Adequate protein intake is crucial for maintaining skin integrity, elasticity, and repair by providing the necessary amino acids for collagen and elastin synthesis. This protein-rich dietary approach aligns well with weight loss goals, as protein promotes satiety, reduces appetite, and helps preserve lean muscle mass, all critical factors for successful and sustainable weight management.
