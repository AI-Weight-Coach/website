Core Benefits of Strength TrainingBuilds muscle mass, boosts resting metabolism, improves bone density, enhances functional movement, and aids chronic disease prevention.
Designed Workout SummaryA full-body routine, approximately 60 minutes (including warm-up/cool-down), moderate difficulty, utilizing dumbbells (or resistance bands) and bodyweight.
Upper Body & Core ExercisesWarm-up (5-10 min dynamic stretches). Exercises: Push-up (3 sets x 8-12 reps), Dumbbell Row (3 sets x 10-12 reps/arm), Plank (3 sets x 30-60 sec hold).
Lower Body ExercisesGoblet Squat (3 sets x 10-12 reps), Reverse Lunge (3 sets x 10-12 reps/leg).
Shoulder, Rest & Cool-downDumbbell Overhead Press (3 sets x 10-12 reps). Rest: 60-90 seconds between sets. Cool-down: 5-10 min static stretches.

How Effective Is Strength Training for Weight Loss?

Strength training is highly effective for weight loss, not just by burning calories during the workout but primarily by increasing lean muscle mass. Muscle is metabolically active, burning more calories at rest than fat, which significantly boosts your resting metabolic rate for sustainable weight management. It also profoundly improves body composition, creating a leaner, more toned physique even if scale weight changes are gradual.
