General Guidelines for Pregnancy ExerciseConsult your healthcare provider before starting or continuing any exercise program. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week. Listen to your body, stay hydrated, and avoid activities with a high risk of falling, abdominal trauma, or lying flat on your back after the first trimester. Maintain a conversational pace during exercise.

Are Pregnancy-Safe Exercises Effective for Weight Loss?

While calorie burn from pregnancy-safe exercises contributes to energy expenditure, their primary role is not typically for significant weight loss during pregnancy. The focus should be on maintaining fitness, promoting a healthy pregnancy, and preparing the body for labor and postpartum recovery. Any weight changes during pregnancy should be guided by a healthcare provider, as appropriate weight gain is crucial for fetal development.
