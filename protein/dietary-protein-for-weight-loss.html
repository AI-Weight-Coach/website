Is Dietary Protein an Effective Strategy for Weight Loss?

Adequate dietary protein is highly effective for weight loss. It promotes satiety, helping to reduce overall calorie intake, and crucially aids in preserving lean muscle mass during a calorie deficit, which is vital for maintaining metabolic rate. Furthermore, protein requires more energy to digest and metabolize (higher thermic effect) compared to carbohydrates and fats, slightly increasing daily calorie expenditure.
