⚠ Important Medical Notice

AI Weight Coach is a wellness app, not a medical device. The information provided is for general educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to be, and should not be used as, a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

1. Not Medical Advice

AI Weight Coach provides:

  • General wellness information
  • AI-estimated calorie and nutrition data
  • Personalized coaching based on your inputs
  • AI-powered conversational coach chat
  • Goal tracking and motivation

This information is not medical advice. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional before:

  • Starting any diet or weight loss program
  • Making significant changes to your eating habits
  • Beginning a new exercise routine
  • If you have any medical conditions or concerns

2. Consult Your Doctor

🚑 When to Seek Medical Advice

You should consult a healthcare professional if you:

  • Have diabetes, heart disease, or other chronic conditions
  • Are pregnant, nursing, or planning to become pregnant
  • Have a history of eating disorders
  • Take medications that affect weight or metabolism
  • Experience dizziness, fatigue, or other symptoms while dieting
  • Are under 18 years of age
  • Plan to lose more than 10% of your body weight
  • Even in case of doubt, always consult your doctor

3. AI Limitations

Our AI technology has inherent limitations:

3.1 Food Recognition Accuracy

  • AI food recognition is approximately 85-95% accurate
  • Portion size estimation may vary
  • Homemade or unusual dishes may be less accurately identified
  • Calorie estimates should be treated as approximations

3.2 Personalized Recommendations and Chat

  • AI coaching and chat responses are based on general nutrition principles
  • Recommendations and chat responses do not account for individual medical conditions
  • The AI coach chat provides conversational wellness support, not professional advice
  • The AI does not have access to your complete health history
  • Suggestions should be verified with a healthcare provider

4. Weight Loss Risks

Weight loss, even when done healthily, carries potential risks:

  • ⚠ Nutrient deficiencies - Restricting calories may lead to inadequate vitamin and mineral intake
  • ⚠ Muscle loss - Rapid weight loss can result in losing muscle along with fat
  • ⚠ Metabolic changes - Your metabolism may slow down with calorie restriction
  • ⚠ Gallstones - Rapid weight loss increases the risk of gallstones
  • ⚠ Psychological effects - Dieting can affect mood, energy, and mental health

5. Healthy Weight Loss Guidelines

✓ Recommended Approach

Medical professionals generally recommend:

  • Losing 0.5-1 kg (1-2 lbs) per week
  • Not going below 1,200 calories/day for women or 1,500 for men without medical supervision
  • Eating a balanced diet with all food groups
  • Combining diet with regular physical activity
  • Staying hydrated and getting adequate sleep
  • Focusing on sustainable lifestyle changes, not quick fixes

6. Eating Disorders Warning

⚠ Important

If you have a history of eating disorders (anorexia, bulimia, binge eating disorder, etc.), calorie tracking apps may not be appropriate for you. Please consult with a mental health professional before using AI Weight Coach.

If you are struggling with disordered eating, please seek help:

  • National Eating Disorders Association (US): 1-800-931-2237
  • Beat Eating Disorders (UK): 0808-801-0677
  • Or contact your local mental health services
