Why High-Protein Low-Calorie Recipes Are Great for Weight Loss

High-protein, low-calorie recipes are an effective strategy for weight loss due to their ability to enhance satiety, helping to reduce overall calorie intake without excessive hunger. Protein also requires more energy to digest and metabolize compared to other macronutrients, offering a slight metabolic advantage. Furthermore, sufficient protein intake is crucial for preserving lean muscle mass during calorie restriction, which is vital for maintaining a healthy metabolism and body composition.
